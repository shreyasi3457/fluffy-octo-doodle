Meet Max!

Max is a spirited one-year-old Labrador mix who arrived at the shelter after being found wandering near Highway 178. Despite his rough start, he's all wagging tail and sloppy kisses — this guy holds absolutely zero grudges.

He's a high-energy pup who thrives with plenty of exercise, a yard to run in, and people who love to play. Max has done wonderfully with children and picks up basic commands quickly — he just needs someone patient enough to channel all that enthusiasm into good habits.

If you're looking for a loyal, joyful companion who will make every single day feel like an adventure, Max is your guy. He can't wait to meet you.
